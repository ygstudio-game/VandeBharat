5Cloud Cost Analysis — AWS & GCP

GPU instance turned on only during active inspection. Instances are stopped between sessions. Cloud cost = active GPU hours only.

Step 1 — Calculate Active GPU Hours/Month

## How many GPU hours does one station need per month? Trains per day (busy station) = 20 trains/day Minutes GPU active per train = 20 min (YOLO + OCR + correlation) GPU active per day = 20 × 20 ÷ 60 = 6.67 hrs/day Add warm-up time (cold start) = 20 starts × 2 min ÷ 60 = +0.67 hr/day ────────────────────────────────────────────── Effective GPU hours/day = 7.5 hrs/day Monthly GPU hours (×30 days) = 7.5 × 30 = 225 hrs/month

Step 2 — Cost Calculation Formula

## Monthly Cloud Cost Formula Monthly Cost = GPU hours/month × Hourly rate × (1 − Discount%) Where Discount% by pricing model: On-Demand → 0% discount (pay as you go) Spot / Preemptible → ~70% discount (interruptible, cheapest) Reserved / CUD 1yr → ~37% discount (stable, no interruption) ## Example with AWS g5.2xlarge at $1.212/hr: On-Demand = 225 hrs × $1.212 × (1 − 0.00) = $272.70/month Reserved = 225 hrs × $1.212 × (1 − 0.37) = $172.10/month Spot = 225 hrs × $1.212 × (1 − 0.70) = $81.81/month

Spot / Preemptible Warning: Spot instances can be interrupted mid-session with 2-min notice (AWS) or 30-sec (GCP). A YOLO inference in progress will be killed. Use Spot only for dev/test or if sessions are short and resumable. For production use On-Demand or Reserved.
